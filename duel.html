GRAVITY
DUEL

Race a curving skyline, bend shots around asteroids, grab gravity powers on the run and use them on your friend. Best of three rounds, and each round is a new arena.

  • Bullets fall in your gravity. From the floor your shots arc down, and from the ceiling they arc up. The arc is aimed for you, so where you stand is your aim.
  • Three curved decks. Running downhill speeds you up. Asteroids and gates float between decks as cover, and they bounce you back if you switch decks into them.
  • Limited ammo. 30 rounds. Every 120 m you run earns +8. Crates and asteroids break open into supplies.
  • Shield. Power charges as you run. D/K spends 35 on a shield that parries bullets back.

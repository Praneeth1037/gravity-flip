TWO PILOTS · CURVED DECKS · BENT BULLETS

GRAVITY
DUEL

Race a curving skyline, bend shots around asteroids and hit your friend with gravity itself. Best of three rounds, and each round is a new arena.

  • Bullets fall in your gravity. From the floor your shots arc down, and from the ceiling they arc up. The arc is aimed for you, so where you stand is your aim.
  • Three curved decks. Floor, bridge and ceiling. Running downhill speeds you up. Asteroids and spinning gates float between decks as cover, and they bounce you back if you switch decks into them.
  • Limited ammo. 30 rounds. Every 120 m you run earns +8. Crates and asteroids break open into supplies.
  • Power. Charges as you run. Fly through gold rings for +20. D/K shield (35) parries bullets back at the shooter.
  • Pick 2 gravity powers per pilot. Most of them hurt your rival and wreck obstacles.
